The problem

Revenue leaks out quietly, one small failure at a time

Payments fail

A card gets declined, an OTP times out, a bank has a bad five minutes — and the sale is just gone unless someone follows up fast.

Checkouts get abandoned

A customer gets to the last step and stops. No error, no complaint — just silence, and a cart that never converts.

Invoices go overdue

B2B receivables slip past their due date because nobody's watching every invoice, every day.

Individually, these look small. Across thousands of transactions a month, they add up to real, recoverable revenue — most of it never chased.

How it works

From the moment it fails, to the moment it's fixed

1 Start here

Catches it the instant it happens

Recoup listens directly to your payment gateway in real time — the moment a payment fails, Recoup already knows, before a customer even reloads the page.

2

Figures out why

Insufficient funds, a declined card, a failed OTP, a bank outage — Recoup tells these apart, because the right fix depends entirely on the reason.

3

Decides if it's worth acting on

Every case is weighed against what it would actually cost to chase it. Recoup won't spend more trying to recover ₹20 than the ₹20 is worth.

4

Reaches out, on the right channel

A fresh payment link over WhatsApp, a personal email, or — for high-value or repeat cases — a handoff to a real person on your team.

5

Gets smarter every time

Recoup tracks what actually worked and adjusts its own assumptions — the longer it runs, the better it gets at picking the right move.
